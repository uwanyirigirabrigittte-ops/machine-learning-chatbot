Rating: 5.0/5.0 | Date: 12 13, 2010 | Helpful votes: 11
Heading: Good package deal, might not be pricey in the long run
I bought this because I needed the 1 1/4 fine screws. A pack of 100 would have been more than enough and less than 1/3 the price. Getting 150 of those screws is worth more, but I'm not likely to need them any time soon. But I am likely to need another size at some point. Or I might find that having the screws around would make life easier. Think of this kit in terms of what you get compared to what you'd pay for your minimum need now, and four screws in two different sizes that you might need later.If you expect to build a new kitchen and will need 500 screws of the same size, then individual sizes are a better option. If you have only occasional need for pocket joinery and are sure you will never use more than one type or thickness of wood, then a single small package of screws would be better. But chances are you will need other sizes for occasional projects and even a one time need will justify the cost of having four more sizes to select from. You've probably also had projects where pocket joinery would have been the best solution, but you would not have considered it unless you had the screws.The best use of this kit is for projects you don't anticipate, for wood you don't typically use, and for times when running to the store or waiting for the package is not worth it. If you did that each time you needed a handful of a different size, you'd end up spending double what this package costs, or drilling dowel holes, clamping, or picking another alternative instead. Plus you'd end up with fewer screws.

--- REVIEW 6 ---
Rating: 5.0/5.0 | Date: 09 12, 2013 | Helpful votes: 7
Heading: Great assortment - here's what you get
Here is exactly what is in the box:NOTE: The product description says the screws come "in five sizes." That's a bit misleading; it sounds like you are getting five different lengths. You are actually getting only THREE lengths (for three sizes of stock); two of the lengths come in both course and fine threads. So you get five varieties, but only three lengths.Course threads are for use in soft woods (pine - including hemlock, MDF, particleboard, etc). Fine threads are for hardwood (maple, oak, etc).For 3/4" stock, 1.25" long screws, bright finish:- 150# SML-F125, Maxi-Loc head #7 shank, fine threads, for 3/4" hard stock- 150# SML-C125, Maxi-Loc head #8 shank, course threads, for 3/4" soft stockFor 1/2" stock, 1" long screws, bright finish:- 150# SPS-F1, Pan Head, #6 shank, fine threads, for 1/2" hard stock- 150# SPS-C1, Pan Head, #7 shank, course threads, for 1/2" soft stockFor 1.5" ("two-by") stock, 2.5" long screws, Blue-Kote finish:- 75# SML-C250B, Maxi-Loc head, #8 shank, course threads, for 1.5" soft stockMaxi-Loc heads have a molded-in "washer" which increases the surface area of the head compared to the Pan Head variety. All screws use the same size square-bit driver."Bright" finish means electroplate-galvanized zinc. This finish won't stand up to long term exposure to adverse weather. Blue-Kote finish (used only in the screws for 1.5" stock) is weather resistant. As the name suggests, these screws are blue.The case is sturdy and should last for years, and it REALLY adds a lot of value to the kit. The locking tabs are nice and tight - if you drop the case it is unlikely to pop open and mix up your screws on the floor. My only complaint is that the case does not have a provision to hold the square-drive bit. A "bit compartment" would make it a little more useful (and an included bit even more so - an extra bit would be a nice bit of insurance).Kreg OEM screws are the highest quality. You should always set your drill clutch to a reasonable torque (not much more than it needs to be for your stock), but you are unlikely to twist the head off of these screws or strip out the square socket if your clutch is too tight.I'll be buying a bunch more fine screws for 3/4" hard stock, which is my most common requirement. The other screws I will need much less frequently, but when you need an oddball screw, it's a bummer to not have it. The set makes no attempt to include screws for REALLY oddball stock sizes (how many people really use 1" stock?)This is a good and generous assortment of high-quality screws for the most common stock sizes used in cabinet-style joinery. An excellent value.(if you find my review helpful, please "like" it. thanks!)
