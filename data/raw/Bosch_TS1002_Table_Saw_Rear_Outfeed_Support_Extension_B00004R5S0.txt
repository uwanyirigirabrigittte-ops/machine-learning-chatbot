Heading: Must have for large pieces
Bolts on easily. Supports well after the cross bar is redrilled to align the support with the table. It is 1/2" low to the table when at full extension. Only one thumbscrew included for locking position but both brackets are drilled and tapped for it.Must be careful when cutting 4x8 sheets as the saw can tip with this support at full extension and the board hanging off it.

Heading: Not quite right
If you need an outfeed support for your Bosch table saw, this is it. There are not really any other alternatives.The design looks pretty good and the price is reasonable. The problem is in the manufacturing. Tolerances are WAY out of line.In order for this accessory to be usable, expect to drill & tap new 1/4-20 holes for the friction clamps, then order new 1/4-20 thumb screws to replace the ridiculously tiny (and VERY loose) screws that came with the unit.If you do this, the Outfeed support will function well.These issues are not what you expect for a tool with the Bosch name on it, not even close.Dan

--- REVIEW 13 ---
Rating: 4.0/5.0 | Date: 12 30, 2008 | Helpful votes: 1
Heading: Works great, but small problem on the 4100DG-09
Took about 10 minutes to install by the directions for the 4100. (The 4000 is mounted slightly differently). Works very well and smooth as silk if you don't tighten the screws on the mounts until the final step.The only problem I've had (really just a annoyance), is that with the Digital Scale for the 4100DG-09, or the Digital Scale mounted as an after accessory, the left rod is stopped by the digital scale when it is in the parked position as specified by Bosch.What that means is that you have to be careful not to slam the extension into ITS parked position or you might damage the Digital scale. You need to stop it a little early and lock it into position to avoid touching the scale.Other than that annoyance, it works quite well!
