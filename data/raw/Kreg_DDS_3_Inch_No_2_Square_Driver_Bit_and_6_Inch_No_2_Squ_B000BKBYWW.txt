Rating: 5.0/5.0 | Date: 12 6, 2008 | Helpful votes: 20
Heading: Cheap and effective - I love the long bit
It's counterintuitive, but the long bit is actually the easiest to use and most stable when driving pocket screws. The Kreg bits are very well made, hardened, and don't wear as fast as ordinary square drive bits. That's particularly important when doing pocket hole construction - there's nothing more frustrating than a stripped bit or screw head halfway in a pocket hole. In fact, I keep my Kreg bits separate and only use them for pocket holes. I use my regular bit set square drive bits for other square drive uses and toss them as they wear.

These bits come with the Kreg pocket hole joinery system and work great for that. Due to the steep angle at which the Kreg jig cuts the pocket hole, you need fairly long R2 bits too keep the face of the chuck off the material to be joined.
