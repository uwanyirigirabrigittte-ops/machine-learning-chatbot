Rating: 1.0/5.0 | Date: 12 26, 2011 | Helpful votes: 51
Heading: I read the manual
The supplied manual(for their 3 small angle grinders) on page 7 clearly states you need a type 1 guard when using 4.5" cutting wheels. This tool only comes with a type 27 guard (good for grinding and sanding). Also, on page 13 of the manual it states:"Warning: A closed, 2-sided cutting wheel guard is not included with this tool but is required when using cutting wheels. Failure to use proper flange and guard can result in injury resulting from wheel breakage and wheel contact".The only guards I can find for sale here on Amazon are for large angle grinders. On Dewalt's site you can get a 4.5" type 1 guard for $10.73 + $10.50 shipping (no thanks).As of this writing, the product description page here does not mention anything about needing a type 1 guard for use with cutting wheels, and lists cutting wheels in 5 places:Under "frequently bought together" two cutting wheels are listed.Under "What Other Items Do Customers Buy After Viewing This Item?" a cutting wheel is listed.Under "Customers Who Bought This Item Also Bought" the first two items are cutting wheels.Under "Related items" cutting wheels are listed."Customer reviews" are full of people talking about cutting with this tool, no one mentions the guards.

Rating: 5.0/5.0 | Date: 06 7, 2007 | Helpful votes: 33
Heading: "My first grinder"
I've used grinders at work but I've never owned one of my own. Now dewalt may not be top dog when it comes to technology or enervation (like Bosch or Panasonic) but they usually make simple, tough and reasonably affordable tools. That's really about all the counts. Most grinders I've used seems to be about the same in design so I didn`t care much about witch I picked as long as it was a good name brand. I started my tool collection off with dewalt long ago so that's what I have stuck with. I like that paddle switch on this one, they are fragile but easy to work with. This particular one is self off locking, there is a little bit you have to rotate to push the switch down that`s on the switch. It was a little strange at first but quickly became comfortable to do. Also this one can be held on, as most can, simple click the paddle and it turns off. The only thing I don't like is that if you where to drop it that locking mechanism on the paddle part of the switch may brake... The handle is a simple screw in, screw out deal that you can put on ether side of it. I don't need to cut structural members all day so the 10 amps, 11K rpm and 4-" size are plenty for round the shop jobs. Fast and powerful enough to cut a car frame but not to fast to use mild "scotch bright" type abrasives if need be. This one has a quick change threaded washer witch can be use for cutting disks or grinding disk without needing a backing ring or spacer for proper clamping and centering.The case has provisions for holding disks (7/8" hole), right now I've got four 1/8" cutting, one grinding and two flap wheels in the holder. The tool still fits level in the case but any more and it might not. I don't think screw on accessories are made to fit in the holder (as it has a 5/8 thread), but there is room in the case for a crimp brush or what ever else wont fit in the disk holder.Over all not a bad package for a hundred bucks or so.

--- REVIEW 3 ---
Rating: 5.0/5.0 | Date: 12 3, 2007 | Helpful votes: 21
Heading: Dewalt Grinder
I love this tool. I wish I would have bought one sooner. I bought this grinder with a specific purpose. To make rounded cuts in 1/2 floor tile. I bought a Dewalt Diamond Blade and this tool at the same time. I like the balance of the tool, being able to move the handle to either side and being able to adjust the shield for various applications. I thought it was going to be awkward cutting the arches in the tile but found the tool easy to handle and with enough power to make the job relatively easy. I made two passes even thought the directions indicated that 1/2" depth was acceptable in one pass. I just wanted to do two passes so the tile didn't break in the wrong spot. Definitely wear goggles, a mask and ear protection as this tool creates a fine dust storm. Dust collection would be a great asset to this tool but I am not quite sure how it could be done without diminishing the tool's flexibility. You will also need a compressor with an air attachment or compressed air to blow out the tool periodically during use to get the grit out. In the directions they recommended wearing gloves because of excessive vibration but I did not find this to be a factor in cutting the tile. They also "required" you to have a double-sided additional shield (not included) when using a cutting blade. They should really include this in the kit as I didn't know I needed it until after the purchase (they should have also put this on the package with the cutting blade). Overall this tool performed better than I expected.

Rating: 1.0/5.0 | Date: 05 3, 2013 | Helpful votes: 16
Heading: Excellent grinder - poor carry case - missing wrench
Typical Dewalt power tool - great tool and crappy case. Liked that the motor cooling is protected from the debris created when grinding. With masonry this will do a lot to keep the motor running longer. Cord is average length so it can often be used without an extension cord on a work bench. Performance is excellent with high RPM's maintained even under a heavy load. Side handle is seldom needed for this small a grinder but it is there should you want it.The case is typical DeWalt. How their product managers can not be embarrassed with their cheap cases is hard for me to understand. Even Makita makes better cases. The latches are the bottom end made in China ones that cost 25 cents. For 50 cents a decent quality latch could have been used and who would care if the kit cost $2 more? I want to be able to easily close a case and latch it and pick it up to carry it without a lot of effort or wondering when the latches will fail and the case will open and everything inside will fall out. For some reason Milwaukee Tools can make excellent cases economically and provide them with their power tools but DeWalt seems unable to do so.DeWalt has a 90 days return for full credit but this does not work if you buy the tool through Amazon which as a 30-day return period so if you are buying it for yourself or as a gift be sure to open it up and make sure that every part for the kit is actually in the case and that the tool works perfectly.I ordered two of these kits and both of them were missing the wrench needed to tighten down the threaded clamp nut that holds the grinding wheel in place. The wrench shows in the parts diagram but was missing from both of the DeWalt D28402K kits. Without this wrench the grinder can only be used with a wire brush and not a grinding wheel, including the wheel included in the kit. I phoned DeWalt and they will readily ship out these wrenches but until in the meantime the tool is not usable. If you have an immediate need for a grinder to use these are not a good choice.
