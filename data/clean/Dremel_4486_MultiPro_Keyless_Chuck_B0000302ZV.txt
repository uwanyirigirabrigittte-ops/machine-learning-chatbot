Heading: It's time for you to "chuck" the collets... I consider the MultiPro chuck to be the best accessory ever made for the Dremel tool. When you're doing work that requires constant bit changes, the changing of collets to fit a bit of a particular diameter becomes a real frustration, and wastes time. So, needless to say, an adjustable chuck is a real boon for these kinds of jobs. Just loosen it to remove one bit, pop in another, tighten, and vwah-lah! The collet nut and collets become pretty much obsolete.The chuck may seem somewhat pricey, but when you consider the time you'll save and the convenience you'll enjoy, it's well worth the extra investment.`Late

--- REVIEW 8 ---
Rating: 3.0/5.0 | Date: 12 20, 2011 | Helpful votes: 8
Heading: Useful but not for precision work
I use this chuck on a Black & Decker RTX-B drill. It fits perfectly and makes changing bits of different diameter very easy. However, I find this chuck not to be a complete replacement for a set of collets. It makes the drill bit wobble a little and at high speeds this makes the drill vibrate nasty if not dangerously! It is nice for what it was made for: quickly changing between relatively slow turning accessories. If you want to use fast spinning drill bits and want accuracy (drilling 0.7mm holes on PCB's) this is NOT the tool for you...
