It is not whisper quiet, but it is not very loud, either.Showed this to a fellow who does a lot of remodeling work commercially and he immediately liked it and intends to buy one.Yeah a Fein is probably better, but not 5-10X better. Rating: 4.0/5.0 | Date: 04 11, 2011 | Helpful votes: 4
Heading: best deal for a cheap tool
I have used this product over 3 times. The quality is just decent. I used the product to cut a square in my cabinet and also to plane the bottom on the door. When trying to cut a square in wooden cabinets, the tool is very hard to control to make a straight light . Since there is no line guide and this is a one speed, the results will be far from professionally for cutting long lines in wood.One hack is to use a variable speed pedal (similar to a dremel or sewing machine) to vary the speed if slowing down helps. Also, the attachments use the same configuration as BOSCH (which owns dremel). There is a free dremel adapter to be able to use any other manufacture's blades on the genesis tool. Then again, the goal was to buy a cheap tool. Some expensive blades cost more than this tool itself.Lastly, for planing the bottom of the door, this tool worked well. Just make sure you put shims on the floor and protect your floor tiles with some steel. I used the plunge cut to cut 1/4 in off the ground. The results were good given I didn't need to remove the door.I think I got my money's worth for this tool. Good for cutting wood and sanding. Sands better than my tiny dremel.
