Rating: 1.0/5.0 | Date: 02 2, 2012 | Helpful votes: 14
Heading: battery contacts are the Achilles heel of this tool
I've owned four of these saws, and so far, the copper battery contacts have distorted and failed on three of them. I'm leery of using the fourth saw, lest the same happen to it. Unfortunately the copper fingers that contact the battery are part of the power controller and trigger mechanism; you can't buy the replacement battery contacts separately. This power controller part is used on over a dozen Dewalt products, most of which draw less current than a recipro saw does. A replacement part will run at least $60 by the time you get it delivered; if you pay for installation, it comes to more than a new bare-tool saw!The saw itself is very powerful, though it vibrates more than a comparable Milwaukee Sawzall. I now use a28 volt Milwaukee Sawzallinstead, which has battery contacts that won't distort under use.I believe the reason I have seen more failure of the battery contacts than other reviewers is that I often changed to a second or third battery while the tool was hot from use. But my feeling is that a recipro saw is made to be pushed hard. All the rest of this Dewalt's specs can take the abuse, but the power controller, which apparently was designed for lesser tools, cannot.
