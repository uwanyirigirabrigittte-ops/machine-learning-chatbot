--- REVIEW 8 ---
Rating: 4.0/5.0 | Date: 01 25, 2012 | Helpful votes: 1
Heading: Kreg Face Clamp
I have been using the Kreg pocket hole jig for years. I like the system, and just used some adjustable C-clamps to hold the jig down. That worked okay, but this is better. One of the jaws snaps into the Kreg jig, so the clamp and the jig stay together. This saves time as you quickly move from one joint to the next. This is also a great general clamp that has many uses around the shop.My only complaints are that the blue handles seem to be ill-fitting, and move around more than they should. It would work just as well without the rubber grips.Second, when you use the clamp only one side of the jaw fits into the Kreg jig. That is fine, however when you are assembling face frames the clamp is upside down which makes it harder to release with one hand.Overall, I really like that it connects to the jig and have been pleased with this clamp.I would recommend it to anyone who uses Kreg jigs.

This face clamp is a bit pricey, but is necessary when using a pocket hole jig. Pocket hole screws tend to move or lift the receiving board up when joining two boards.
