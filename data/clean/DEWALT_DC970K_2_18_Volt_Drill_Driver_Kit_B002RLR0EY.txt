Rating: 2.0/5.0 | Date: 01 28, 2014 | Helpful votes: 5
Heading: Could have been five stars
This is a powerful drill compared to my old Ryobi rechargeable. I am quite happy with the performance of this tool. It plows a 3/8" bit through hardwood easily. Having it come with two batteries is a nice plus too.I did however run in to a couple of issues.The first thing that happened was I inadvertently switched speeds while the drill was still spinning and from then on the drive sounded different - not sure what happened. It still works fine and runs smoothly but the drive makes a slightly different sound. Though this was my doing, it does indicate to me a cheaply made gear drive mechanism.Something else was made obvious while I was doing some precision work. I was drilling for small hinges on a cabinet and noticed that the drill bit was wobbling. I had not noticed this before when using larger bits because it isn't as obvious. As it turns out, the chuck wobbles significantly enough that I had trouble holding the tip of a 5/64" bit on center to start a hole.I have requested a return/replacement on this drill because of the wobble. I have Googled the chuck wobble issue and found that other people have had the same problem with this drill. Unless I have more misfortune with my replacement, I will leave this review with five stars. All manufacturers have hiccups now and then - I'll give DeWalt a chance to sort this out. My advice to all is to check your chucks if you can check chucks and may all chuck checks check ok.*** UPDATE ***I received my replacement drill. The new one appears to be true and wobble free - as it should be. Ensuring they make a drill with a straight chuck would seem to be the most basic quality inspection on a drill. I guess they save a few yen by sending some out crooked. These are priced higher then many of the competitor's drills so they should be better quality. Since initially posting this review, I've found lately that DeWalt isn't afraid to put their name on sub standard power tools and hand tools. I purchased their screwdriver set a month ago and had to return it because they were already wearing out and two of the handles started slipping. Like many others, if it said DeWalt on the tool I automatically assumed it would be excellent quality. Not so anymore... This is unfortunate for the Dewalt name.I deducted two stars for lack of quality control and one star for ruining the DeWalt name for me.
